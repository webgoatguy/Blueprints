    How to play

    Red vs Blue on an endless hex board. Each player owns three blueprints: 7-cell stamps that sit beside the board and can be used again and again.

    Start

    • Blueprints 1 and 3 (Normal type): a Score cell surrounded by 6 normal cells.
    • Blueprint 2 (Score type): a Score centre ringed by alternating Walls and Score cells, 3 of each. Red's Walls are west, north-east and south-east of the centre; Blue's are the mirror image (east, north-west and south-west), so each side has a Wall facing the other.
    • All six blueprints also start on the board, on one row with one empty tile between neighbours: Blue's on the left, Red's on the right, and the two Score types at the outer ends. Each player starts with 6 Score cells.

    Turn order (a game option)

    • 1 · 2 · 2 · 2 … (default): Blue makes 1 move, then the players alternate 2 moves each: Red 2, Blue 2, Red 2, and so on.
    • 1 · 1 · 1 · 1 …: the players simply take turns one move at a time, Blue first.

    A move is one of

    • Place: stamp one of your blueprints onto the board. It may not overlap any existing cell, yours or your opponent's, and it must touch one of your cells. Every opponent cell touching your new cells is deleted, unless it is a Wall.
    • Edit: move one cell to a new spot in the same blueprint or another of your blueprints. Every blueprint must stay one connected blob. Moved cells keep their type.

    Score

    • Score cells earn nothing on their own. You only score by deleting your opponent's Score cells.
    • Each enemy Score cell you delete pays out the number of Score cells you had on the board before that placement. Score cells in the blueprint you just placed don't count yet.
    • Example: you have 7 Score cells on the board and place a blueprint holding 2 Score cells, deleting 1 enemy Score cell. You get 7 points, not 9. Delete 2 at once and you get 14.
    • The panel shows what your next kill would pay, for example +7/kill. While you aim a placement, the bar at the bottom shows what it would delete and earn.

    Cells

      Playing online

      • Create a game and send the invite link. The first person to open it takes the other seat; anyone after that can watch.
      • You can take back your own last move until your opponent moves.
      • Your seat is remembered on this browser. To continue on another device, use Menu → Copy my rejoin link (keep that one private).

      Controls

      • Click a blueprint to pick it up. The cell you click is the one that lands under your cursor. Click the board to place it (touch: tap to preview, tap again to place).
      • Drag a single blueprint cell to move it (an edit).
      • Drag the board to pan; scroll or pinch to zoom.
      • Esc or right-click cancels · Ctrl/⌘+Z undo / take back · 1–3 pick a blueprint · F fit view.
